FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about mobile repairs, warranties, or repair duration? We have answers.

How long does a typical display or battery replacement take?
Screen replacements and battery replacements for popular models (iPhone, Samsung, OnePlus, Xiaomi) typically take only 30 to 45 minutes. Complex chip-level and water damage repairs may take 2 to 4 hours.
Do you provide warranty on repaired phones?
Yes! KK Mobile Shop provides an official 90-day warranty on all replaced hardware parts, including screens, batteries, charging ports, and IC chips. If the part fails under normal use, we replace it free of charge.
Will my personal data remain safe during the repair?
Absolutely. We respect your complete privacy. We never access your photos, messages, or apps. For hardware repairs (display, battery, charging port), your data is 100% untouched. For software flashes, we advise taking a backup if possible.
How does the online Repair ID tracking work?
When you submit a repair request, a unique Repair ID (e.g. KK-78210) is generated. You can enter this ID anytime on our "Track Status" page to view the live 6-stage progress: Received, Under Inspection, Waiting for Parts, Repair In Progress, Ready for Pickup, and Delivered.
What is your "No Fix, No Fee" policy?
If your device is severely damaged (e.g. cracked CPU or beyond economical repair) and we cannot restore functionality, we charge ₹0 for the attempt. You only pay when your phone is repaired.
